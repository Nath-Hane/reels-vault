## Description
1.	Plan tomorrow, tonight. Just 3 priorities. No 20-step morning routine needed.
	2.	Start with an easy win, not your phone. Something small — make tea, stretch, tidy your desk. It sets momentum.
	3.	Sort your food once a week. Batch cook or just decide tomorrow’s meals tonight. It frees up mental space.
	4.	When focus slips, use 20–5. Twenty minutes on, five off. Reset and go again.
	5.	Study in public when you can’t at home. A library, café, anywhere. The environment carries you.
	6.	Take fewer notes. Listen to actually understand. Use your notepad for questions, not just scribbles.
	7.	Keep a ‘done list’. Proof you’re making progress, not just chasing tasks.
	8.	Walk before work. Even 10 minutes clears your head and shifts your mood.
	9.	Ask: “What would the best version of me do right now?” Sometimes it’s rest. Sometimes it’s pushing through. That honesty matters.
	10.	Make your bed. First small win of the day, always.

Momentum is such an underrated superpower during the chaos of uni life and productivity. Using even 1 of these habits always made the tasks easier to do.
