## Description
Here’s volume 2 of my photography technique tips series - with some of my favorites like frames, light & shadow, panning & blur, minimalism, and sun stars. Like I’ve said before, capturing great images while traveling can be overwhelming. You want to capture not only just what you saw, but how you felt. And if you missed volume 1, definitely head over to my grid. 

This series was born out of an old habit I used to improve and diversify my photography. I’d keep a short list in my phone of at least 15 techniques or shot types I could use to capture different angles and elements of the places I visited. Not every type would fit every scene, but by consistently practicing them, they became second nature. Now, I can approach each scene more organically and the right ones just click in my mind. 

And if you’re new here, welcome!

I’m Rohan Ugale, and I’ve been exploring & photographing Japan for years. 

If you’re hoping to find out about interesting & smaller places to see, plus learn to make photos you love, you’re in the right place!
