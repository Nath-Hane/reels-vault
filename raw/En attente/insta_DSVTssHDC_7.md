## Transcription audio
Dip the bags in melted chocolate. Add ice cubes and chill for 30 minutes. Mix the eggs with sugar. Sift the flour with cocoa and baking powder. Pour the batter into the pan and bake for 15 minutes. Cut the sponge cake into pieces. Mix the cream cheese with powdered sugar and whipped cream. Mix with melted chocolate. Prepare the caramel and mix with toasted peanuts. Fill the chocolate molds with sponge cake, chocolate cream, and caramel.
