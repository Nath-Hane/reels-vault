## Description
What EVERYONE forgets… 🔻

Stop saying you’re not flexible. Anyone can instantly improve their flexibility. The only reason people think they’re not flexible is because they’re stretching wrong.

If you’re serious about recovering faster, staying injury free, mastering skills and gaining strength - you have to be doing these things right:

1️⃣ STRETCHING BEFORE TRAINING: If you want to increase soreness, injury risk and reduce force output - you should absolutely stretch before training.

You need to be warmed up to stretch safely and effectively - otherwise your muscles will be sensitive and less pliable (stretchy), making it much harder for them to lengthen and to improve your ROM.

Stretching after training will sustain a high blood flow to the muscles you trained, providing the nutrients necessary to repair and recover, whilst the heat produced from contractions will make it much easier and less painful to stretch your muscles.

The only stretches that don’t negatively impact your workout are dynamic stretches, which will help prepare your muscles.

2️⃣ STRETCHING WITHOUT STRENGTHENING: Your muscles are like blue tack. Stretch a very thin piece, it’ll quickly snap or get damaged. When it is thick and strong, it can stretch much further.

Weak muscles can be flexible - but accessing these ranges of motion is unsafe without the strength necessary to prevent them from overstretching.

This is the reason hypermobile athletes are much more likely to get injured. ALWAYS build strength alongside flexibility to stay safe and progress fast.

3️⃣ INCREASING FLEXIBILITY: If you stretch but don’t gain flexibility, it’s not you - it’s how you stretch.

Make sure you’re always taking slow deep breaths during your stretch, that you avoid painful ranges of motion, and stretch for at least a total of one minute for each stretch (doesn’t have to be in one set).

Do this consistently, and you’ll GUARANTEE progress.
