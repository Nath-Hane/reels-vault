Italian Coffee Cream in 5 minutes ☕  it’s smooth, creamy and a dream coffee lover’s come true with only 3 ingredients 🙂 

Ingredients:

-36 gr of espresso from Moka pot or instant coffee 
-400 ml of heavy whipping cream
-25 g of sugar 
-Cocoa Powder (optional)
-Add everything into a jar and shake it for 5 min until you hear an slapping sound
