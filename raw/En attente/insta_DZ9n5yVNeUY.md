## Description
A lot of people have been asking me how I learned Chinese, what resources I used, and how I knew what to study next 

So I decided to put everything into one notion template and create the Chinese Fluency Roadmap 🇨🇳

It’s basically the study system I wish I had when I first started learning Chinese! ☺️

Inside you’ll find:
✨ Personalized study planners & roadmaps for HSK 1–6
✨ HSK resources, mock tests, flashcards, audio, and answer keys
✨ Reading, writing, listening, and speaking practice materials
✨ Progress trackers to keep you accountable
✨ A Chinese Immersion Hub with donghua, dramas, films, books, podcasts, games, and more

Whether you’re starting from zero or already studying for the HSK, you’ll have a clear roadmap showing you exactly what to study next
