## Transcription audio
Adobe is officially dead. Their biggest competitor, Affinity, just made their entire suite free. Sounds too good to be true, right? Here's the whole story. For decades, Adobe owned design. Photoshop, Illustrator, and Design, they were the only game in town. But in 2015, a small UK company called Serif did something radical. They built Affinity. Same power as Adobe, but it's a one-time purchase instead of an endless subscription. Freelancers and studios naturally started switching. People were tired of paying Adobe every month with little improvement and no escape. Affinity became Adobe's first real competitor. Now here's where it gets interesting. Adobe actually doubled down on their subscription login. More price tags and more features bundled behind higher tiers. That left an opening. Canva, the design platform for beginners, realized this opportunity. They surely dominated their existing market, but the pros wanted something deeper. So in March 2024, Canva made their biggest move ever. They bought Affinity for $380 million, but then something very unexpected happened recently. Instead of keeping it exclusive or expensive, Canva merged all the Affinity apps, photo designer, and publisher into one unified platform and made it completely free forever. AI tools are sold separately and that's fair. But all the base features are yours for free with no strings attached. Adobe subscription model just broke and if you are not locked into it, there's no real reason to stay anymore.
