## Description
Rustic Berry Envelope Tart 🍓 because I like pretty things 🥹 I made a whole wheat dough and filled it with raspberry preserves, sliced strawberries, and whole raspberries. You could use store bought pie pastry as well and cut into a 12” square. Fold like above, brush with an egg wash, sprinkle with Demerara sugar, bake at 400 degrees F for 20 minutes. 

Use a small heart cookie cutter to make the strawberries and edible flowers only please. Xo
