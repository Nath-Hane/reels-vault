🍜Spicy Creamy Miso Ramen
Calories: ~640 kcal | Protein: 70g | Carbs: ~49 | Fat: 22g

🧂 Ingredients (1 serving):
	•	120g cooked yaki soba noodles
	•	1 cup Pacific bone broth + 1 cup water
	•	1.5 tsp white miso paste
	•	1/2 tsp doubanjiang
	•	1/2 tsp lite soy sauce
	•	Sesame oil spray (light mist, ~1/4 tsp worth)
	•	1 clove garlic, minced
	•	1/2 tsp grated ginger
	•	50g 90% lean ground pork
	•	95g cooked chicken thigh, sliced
	•	1 soft-boiled egg
	•	1/2 cup nonfat Greek yogurt
	•	2 tbsp corn
	•	1/4 cup brown beech mushrooms
	•	1/2 cup bok choy, chopped
	•	2 tbsp scallions, sliced
	•	S&B chili powder (Nanami Togarashi), to taste

Directions
1. Soft-boil the egg (6½-7 min). Chill and peel.
2. Cook chicken thigh until cooked through, slice.
3. Sauté garlic + ginger in sesame oil. Brown pork, stir in miso, doubanjiang, soy sauce.
4. Add broth + water. Simmer with corn + mushrooms.
5. Turn off heat, whisk in Greek yogurt.
6. Add noodles to a bowl, pour in broth, top with chicken, egg, veggies, and chili powder
