## Transcription audio
If you're editing your photos in Lightroom Mobile, but not using the tone curve, you're missing out big time. So here is exactly how to use it. The tone curve is broken up into five sections. You have the blacks, the shadows, the mid tones, the highlights, and the whites. I usually place three additional points on the curve, allowing me to control each section. And nine times out of 10, I'll drop the shadows, increase the mid tones and highlights, and slightly raise the blacks. This adds a nice level of contrast to your shot, but by lifting the blacks just slightly at the end, fades out the darker parts of your image, and gives your shot a very nice cinematic feeling.
