If you're incurring multi-passionate, you need to watch these videos. The first one is called why some of us don't have one-true calling. And she talks about the fact that there are a lot of people that are multi-passionate, that are multi-potentialite, that don't have one-true calling because they simply have too many interests. And since we are used to getting asked constantly what we want to do when we grew up, we think that we have and must have one-true calling. But for some people, that simply isn't the case. And so I think it's liberating when you change that mindset. And the second video instead is called how to manage multiple interests.
