## Transcription audio
You've been tucking in your shirts all along, so I'm going to teach you 5 easy ways to tuck your shirt. Classic tuck. Tuck your shirt in normally and raise both of your hands. You'll have a simple, relaxed look. Ralph tuck. Connect the two bottom buttons. Tuck your shirt in. And this is the perfect tuck for a clean layered look. J-Gal tuck. Button the third button from the bottom, and you want to cross the remaining fabric onto the opposite side. And tuck it in. This tuck gives a strong V shape and a clean look. New Mu tuck. Connect the bottom button to the second last hole on the other side. And repeat. And this creates a perfect fold to layer sweaters. The Sawn tuck. Button the last button to the third hole on the other side. And do the same on the other side. And finally connect in both of the remaining buttons. You have a clean tuck that stays in place all day.
