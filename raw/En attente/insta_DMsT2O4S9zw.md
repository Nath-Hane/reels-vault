Garlic Miso Udon 🤎✨
—simple udon recipes 

INGREDIENTS 
- 1-2 pack of udon
- 50gr unsalted butter
- 5-6 garlic cloves
- 1-2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp miso / doenjang
- 1 tsp lemon juice (optional)
- Salt, Pepper, Chicken seasoning powder & MSG

Steps 
First, cook the udon in boiling water for 1–2 minutes, then set it aside. In a pan, melt some butter and sauté garlic until fragrant. Add the udon noodles, then stir in soy sauce, miso & lemon juice. Season to your liking with salt, pepper, chicken seasoning powder, and a pinch of MSG. Enjoy with your protein of choice <3

Note: Be sure to taste as you go, everyone’s preference is different!
