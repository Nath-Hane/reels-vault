## Transcription audio
How did one of the most beautiful, hand-drawn films ever made end up being one of the most overlooked? An Oscar nomination, amazing reviews, yet you still probably haven't seen it. This is day one of talking about some of the most underrated, overlooked, and straight up forgotten animated movies of all time. And today we're going to talk about Wolf Walkers. A story about friendship between two girls. One who hunts wolves, and one who becomes one. It's set in 1600s Ireland, in a town governed by an authoritarian leader. Where a young English girl named Robin arrives with her dad. A hunter, tasked with wiping out the last wolf pack. But when she meets Mabe, a wild girl that can transform into a wolf while she sleeps, everything changes. Robin is torn between her dad's duty to wipe out wolves and her newfound loving friendship with one. The movie explores themes of colonization, nature versus civilization, and the magic of friendship. All wrapped in some of the most breathtaking hand-drawn animation you will ever see. With these insane, stylized visuals that feel like a living tapestry. With swirling lines and vibrant colors that pull you into this folkloric world. A story that makes you feel alive, warm, and nostalgic, while teaching you to find freedom in a world that demands obedience.
