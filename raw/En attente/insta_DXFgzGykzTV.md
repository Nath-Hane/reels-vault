## Description
A recent controvery has emerged on the internet regarding the fact that Witch Hat Atelier Mangaka copied it's many themes from Frieren & Dungeon Meshi.

But this claim falls apart when looking at the release dates of the mangas, which show Witch Hat Atelier predates Frieren by several years. Witch Hat Atelier was seriealized in July 2016,while Frieren was seriealized in April 2020. Witch Hat Atelier had been running for nearly four years before Frieren even released its first chapter.

The confusion likely comes from fans grouping these three titles together as the "Big Three" of Modern Fantasy. Unlike 90s Dark Fantasy mangas like Berserk & the recent ones like Demon Slayer/Made in Abyss, Fantasy mangas like Frieren/Witch Hat Atelier/Dungeon Meshi are wholesome, vibrant & often positively existentialist.

You may also see accusations that the character Qifrey (from Witch Hat Atelier) is a copy of Gojo Satoru (from Jujutsu Kaisen) due to their white hair and blindfold/glasses. This is also false; Qifrey's character design debuted in the manga in 2016, roughly 1-2 years before Gojo became a cultural icon with the JJK anime popularity, and the "blindfolded mentor" trope predates both.
