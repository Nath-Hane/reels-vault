## Description
Dreaming of a fully paid trip to Japan? ✈️ 🇯🇵

I — and hopefully dozens of MIRAI alumni who will soon pop up on your feed — have amazing news for you: applications for the MIRAI Youth Exchange Program 2026/2027 are officially opening! 🎌✨

MIRAI is a prestigious youth exchange program supported by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Japan, bringing together undergraduate & master’s students, researchers, young professionals and future changemakers from around the world 🌍

The goal? To build international friendships, deepen understanding of Japan, exchange ideas, and create a global network of young people shaping the future.

And yes… you actually get to go to Japan 🥹🇯🇵

Depending on your group, you can look forward to meetings with Japanese experts, university visits, tech hubs, cultural experiences, discussions with Japanese youth - and, of course, experiencing Japan beyond the tourist checklist. 🍜⛩️

💴 And the best part?Major expenses are covered by the host side, including international flights, accommodation, meals, domestic transportation in Japan and program activities.

👀 but… can YOU apply?

You may be eligible if you are:

🎓 An undergraduate or master’s student, researcher, or young professional
🎂 20+ years old at the time of the trip
🇬🇧 Comfortable communicating your ideas and experiences in English
🌍 A citizen/resident of an eligible MIRAI country

⚠️ Important: Eligibility, themes, travel dates and application procedures vary depending on your country.
