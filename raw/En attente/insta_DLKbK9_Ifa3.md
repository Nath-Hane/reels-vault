## Description
Doing more plyometrics won’t always make you more explosive.

In fact, without a solid strength base, it could set you back.

Here’s why: 

Plyos are meant to express force — but if you don’t have much strength to begin with, there’s nothing to express.

Especially if you’re doing extreme plyometrics such as bounding or depth jumps, you would usually end up injured and come up with overuse injuries. 

It’s not that plyometrics don’t work. Starting and using plyometrics is key to building athleticism. 

They just need the right foundation to work their magic and you just need to learn how to progress before getting to the more intense ones. 

Start with: 
✅ Foundational strength
✅ Landing mechanics
✅ Joint control and stability
✅ Gradual progressions
