## Transcription audio
This new anime has been 8 years in the making by Kyoto Animation and it looks gorgeous. Sparks of Tomorrow takes place in an alternate world Kyoto in the early 20th century, where steam engines are pretty much the only thing that have advanced and the city is shrouded in smoke. As the story follows Kakachi who is haunted by the loss of his brother who once dreamed of an age of electricity. And it also follows a girl named Inako who is deeply spiritual who has been forced to not only hide her dreams but also her regrets for her late mother. But one day their lives both changed when their paths crossed both seeking out the 20th century electrical catalogue that depicted their dreams. As the two look to change their own lives while challenging a new era as this is a story of renewal in many ways. The anime is based on the light novel that first came out in 2018 with Kyoto Animation working on this who also worked on A Silent Voice and Violet Evergarden. But this anime was announced more than 8 years ago which is one year before the tragic arson attack on the studio. So it's really great to see this studio coming out of all that and still wanting to adapt this story and it looks like they've done an incredible job. Kyoto Animation has done some amazing work in the past and I really hope this new series will get them back to where they once were. Sparks of Tomorrow is set to come out in 2016. So share this with a friend who loves anime.
