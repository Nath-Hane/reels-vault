## Description
NEW YEARS REV is Green Day’s own love letter to chasing impossible dreams. The band didn’t just inspire this story they’re producing it AND appear as themselves in this wild ride about three young punks who travel across the country believing their band is opening for Green Day... only to discover it was all a prank.

Packed with all your favorite Green Day songs and the band’s own punk rock wisdom: “our love letter to all of our favorite road trip movies, ‘90s nostalgia, and chasing it all no matter how ridiculous it gets.” 🎸

Making its world premiere at Toronto International Film Festival September 12th and coming to you soon, so stay tuned! @GreenDay @newyearsrevmovie
