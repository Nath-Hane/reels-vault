🚨 Stop thinking of the bar muscle up as just a pull up! 🚨
If you want to nail that clean transition over the bar, you’ve gotta treat it like a combo move:
🔥 Vertical pull (like a pull up) ➕ Horizontal pull (like a row) = Smooth bar muscle up 💪

Here’s the secret 🧠👇
➡️ The first half is explosive vertical pulling – chest to bar!
➡️ The second half is a powerful horizontal lean – think about pulling your chest behind the bar as you turn your wrists over and drive the elbows back and up! 🚀

This shift in angle is what makes the transition effortless 🌀
Drills like feet-assisted muscle ups, band-assisted transitions, and explosive chest-to-bar pull ups help you build this hybrid pulling strength! 💯

🎯 Stop muscling it. Start flowing it.
Master the angle, control the transition, and own the bar. 🦍🔥
