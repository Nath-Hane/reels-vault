🏠 Home Plyometric Routine
Single-leg hops (side + front/back) to train ELASTICITY ⚡
• 12 seconds each side x 3 sets
• Use anything as markers
• Focus: quick off ground, stay light

Lateral hops-stick landing to train STABILITY 🧠
• 6 reps each side x 3 sets
• Focus: explode off the chair, soft landing, control knees

Explosive bike sprints to train FAST-TWITCH ACTIVATION💥 
• 10–20 sec all out
• Focus: fast + strong pedal drive
• Helps with knee recovery and quad activation

Bulgarian split squat jumps to train  POWER💥
• 8 rep each side x 2 sets
• Focus: explode up, land soft
• Bring the knee high and push with 100% effort each rep
• Trains single knee landing as well

Jump lunges to train COORDINATION ⚡
• 10 reps x 2 sets 
• Focus: smooth switch, stay balanced
• Your glutes will burn after this 🔥 

Do all exercises on both sides ✅
