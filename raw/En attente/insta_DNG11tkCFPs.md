RECIPE

Ingredients:
1 3/4 cups (210g) all-purpose flour 
3/4 tsp baking soda
1/2 tsp salt
1/2 cup (113g) unsalted butter, softened
3/4 cup (165g) light brown sugar
1/4 cup (50g) granulated sugar
1 large egg
2 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
1 1/2 cups (270g) semi-sweet chocolate chips, plus extra for topping

Instructions:
Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a half sheet pan.
In a bowl, whisk together flour, baking soda, and salt.
In another bowl, beat butter until smooth. Add both sugars and mix until combined (about 2 minutes).
Mix in egg and vanilla.
Add dry ingredients and stir until just combined. Fold in chocolate chips.
Press dough evenly into pan. Sprinkle extra chocolate chips on top if you want.
Bake 20–25 minutes, until golden on top. Cool completely before cutting or stacking.
