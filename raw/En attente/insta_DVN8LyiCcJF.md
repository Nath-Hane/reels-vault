## Description
Stop eating sad, plain eggs and make these HOT HONEY EGGS bcs they are fireeee 🔥 

Crispy edges, runny yolk, and a sweet spicy saucy texture that almost reminds you of Teriyaki sauce 😮‍💨

Recipe : (Serves 1)
- Drizzle honey over a hot pan (make sure to use a stick resistant pan to avoid any sticking)
- Put mozzarella cheese on top of the honey (be generous)
- Crack 2-3 eggs & drizzle chilli oil over the eggs
- Let them cook for 2-3 min
- Gently fold the eggs and you’ll see a beautiful caramelised base 
- Turn off the heat 
- Serve with a garnish of chives/spring onion greens & flaky salt✨
