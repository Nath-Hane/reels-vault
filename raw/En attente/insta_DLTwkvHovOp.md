# Video by studywme_2005

## Description
Not Pomodoro. Not active recall— this is next level.
4/30 Levelup Series

1.	The 3-2-1 Cheat Sheet Method
Write down:
• 3 key points
• 2 questions
• 1 analogy or example
Every time you finish a topic. You’ll revise while creating.

⸻

	2.	“Fake Class” Mode
Set your phone up, stand, and teach the wall for 5 minutes.
Add weird diagrams. Explain like you’re making a video.
(You’ll never forget what you taught.)

⸻

	3.	Study Backwards
Open your book from the last chapter. It feels rebellious, and it helps your brain connect dots in reverse. Great for concept-heavy subjects.

⸻

	4.	The “Scribble Storm”
Take a blank sheet, and write everything you know about a topic in 2 minutes without stopping. No notes.
→ Then look back and fill in gaps. Works better than passive reading.

⸻

	5.	Study in 3 Rooms
Change physical location every 45 minutes.
Your memory will attach the topic to the place. Bonus: no burnout from sitting in one spot.

⸻

	6.	Turn Doubts into WhatsApp Voice Notes
Record your doubt/explanation and send it to yourself.
Listen to it before bed. You’ll absorb it like a podcast.

⸻

	7.	“Catch Me If You Can” Timer Game
Set a timer for less than you think you need (e.g. 15 mins for a 25-min task). Try to beat it.
Speed + pressure = high focus. (Gamifies your brain.)

⸻

	8.	Doodle Logic
🎨 For every topic, doodle something weird about it.
Example: CPU = a chef in a kitchen. It doesn’t have to be accurate. It just needs to stick.
