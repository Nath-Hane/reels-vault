## Transcription audio
Most people think that once you can touch your toes, you've reached hamstring flexibility. Except that's not really the case, because you can take it way further than most people even ever try. I'm not saying you need to have the ability to hug your legs as tight as Jesus loves you, or touch your elbow to your toe. But if you do have that ability, you can kick bad guys in the face, or in the very least, ask them to smell your toes. Not sure why you would want to do that, but it is just three steps to get there. Step one, nerve floss for 30 slow reps. This is mostly just to get you warmed up, and to tell your nervous system it's okay to stretch this far. Then active stretch. Basically, find your hamstring stretch, and then press your heel down so the hamstring forces to flex. Do two to three sets of 30 long uninterrupted seconds. Oh, let's go. And lastly, passive stretch, which means you're going to be relaxing your hamstrings instead of flexing them while you stretch. Hold for two to three more sets of barely tolerable, but not painful 30 second sets. Stay flexi.
