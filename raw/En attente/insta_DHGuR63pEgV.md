How to build STRONG knees ↓

1️⃣ Isometric Wall Sits:
Hold a 90-degree wall sit for 2+ minutes
Perfect for strengthening knee tendons and improving joint stability

2️⃣ Deep Squat Jumps:
Start from a deep squat, jump explosively, and land softly
Boost lower body power and explosive strength

3️⃣ Dynamic ATG Lunges:
Alternate sides with heels hovering
Build mobility and control while deepening your squat range

4️⃣ Hops:
Hop on the balls of your feet
Enhance foot, ankle, and knee strength for better stability

5️⃣ Depth Jumps:
Begin with a small height, progressively increase
Improve impact absorption and enhance explosive power in your legs

## Transcription audio
Here's how to transform your knees from weak to strong. You can get started with the wall sit hold, keeping your knees bent at 90 degrees, and work up to at least 2 minutes, then move on to this harder version and repeat. This will get your knee tendons nice and stiff, and if you do squat jumps, starting each jump from the deepest squat possible, jumping as high as possible, and landing as softly as possible. You'll develop power and tendon elasticity. You can also do a split squat variation of this, where you stay on the balls of your feet and lunge forward, descending with control into a deep split squat, keeping both of your heels hovering, and you can't forget about hopping, the lowest hanging fruit. I urge you to do more hopping throughout the week, because this is straight-up medicine for your ankles and feet fascia, which will only help your knees perform better. And if you have no knee pain, you can get started with depth jumps, practicing soft landings, incrementally increasing the height from which you jump. I want you to get started with these once a week, eventually getting to 2-3 times a week, and I promise your knees will feel bouncier and more elastic than ever before.
