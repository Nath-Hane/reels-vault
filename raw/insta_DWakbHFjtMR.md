# Video by langstudies

## Description
How I learn multiple languages at the same time, without EVER mixing them up...⁠
⁠
YES, it’s possible.⁠
No, you don’t need to be a genius.⁠
But there are things to keep in mind!⁠
Here’s what works (and what will save your sanity):⁠
⁠
⁠
1. Don’t start two new languages at once.⁠
This is rule #1.⁠
If your newest language isn’t at least B1 (low-intermediate), don’t add another one yet.⁠
⁠
2. Choose languages that are very different. (at the start)⁠
Spanish & Italian? Norwegian & Swedish? Be so real with yourself, you’re bound to mix those up.⁠
Instead, go for contrast:⁠
⁠
French + Japanese⁠
German + Korean⁠
Arabic + Portuguese⁠
⁠
(After you’ve really nailed down a language, you can totally learn another one that’s similar.)⁠
⁠
3. Assign each language a different intensity.⁠
Here’s what I do:⁠
⁠
🌱 Maintenance mode – Just 15 min a day (immersion)⁠
☘️ Gradual improvement – 30 min a day (casual focused study)⁠
🌳 Turbo focus – 1–2 hours daily, deeper work⁠
⁠
This keeps your energy focused while making gradual gains across all your langs.⁠
⁠
⁠
4. Consume different types of content in each one.⁠
This usually happens naturally because every culture shines in different genres:⁠
⁠
Japanese = anime/game shows⁠
Spanish = soap operas⁠
Korean = k-dramas/reality tv⁠
French = cinema⁠
⁠
Associate each language with a specific genre or vibe.⁠
It creates strong mental links and helps distinguish the languages even more in your brain.⁠
⁠
Which languages are you learning right now? Do you find them easy to juggle? ⁠
⁠
