## Description
Joe McNallys book taught me this technique that allows you to get away with waaaay slower shutter speeds than thought before. 

It's all about how you hold the camera. Here are some other tips not mentioned in the video.

1. Keep everything tight. Keeping your elbows tucked in allows the camera to be closer to your center of mass, allowing a steadier shot. 

2. Take a breath and slowly press the shutter. breathing out as you take the photo will improve steadiness, as well as slowly pressing the trigger. Quick pressing will do you no good here.

Other things to mention: You need to use your left eye for this method to look through the viewfinder. some people prefer their right eye, so this might not be for you.  personally, I shoot with my tight eye, but I don't have any issues doing this, I find it quite comfortable, actually. 

The link sends you to an online version of this book, I've read it, and for photographers that are trying to get into flash setups, it's personally been a game changer.
