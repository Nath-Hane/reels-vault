## Description
Which design would you wear? 👀

Kimonos are quite literally being thrown away in Japan. With a HUGE decline in use most 500 tons sit in closets or end up in the trash because of a low resale value despite being made of materials like silk. 

BUT @etsuka_fashion is creating custom works of art with SO many different kinds of garments. From Kimono hoodies, jackets, pants, skirts, suit jackets and even Chinese knot jackets their handcrafted designs are incredibly creative and high quality. 

@kamilas4am_mimi and @harry_kamilas4am have created such a warm and vibrant community through Etsuka, saving these valuable Japanese garments by repurposing them into beautiful pieces. I couldn’t recommend their stuff more!
