# Video by elodieintoulouse

## Description
The cocktail bar only locals or very well prepared visitors know 🍸👌🏼

A few meters away from place du Capitole this small and intimist cocktail bar is one of Toulouse’s best kept secret. 

As soon as you enter, you forget about the buzzing atmosphere from the city. It’s soft, chic and elegant.
The cocktails are the most creatives I have seen and tasted in a while and delicious. And the staff very professional and clearly know their subject 👌🏼

It’s the type of place I love going to when I need a little bit of spark from big cities life ✨

Save it for your trip or night out!
