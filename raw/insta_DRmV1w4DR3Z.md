# Video by emilyrosecharlotte

## Description
I haven’t exercised in 6 years.
See how stupid that sounds?

You can be a sporty person, and do lots of sport. 
We all know that.

Many people aren’t sporty people, and still exercise…. Because it is good for you. 

And yet!!

« I haven’t read a book since school » 

Is worn with such pride!

You do not have to be a reader or a book worm or a nerd or « into littérature » to read. 
I would argue reading is for the mind what exercise is for the body.

Your heart will keep beating for a while without both.
But without both, your life will be dull, un-stretched, unlived.

« But I don’t know what to read. »

Read it all!! 

You weren’t born listening to  your Spotify favourites on shuffle, you tailored your likes. 
It’s the same.

And since I’m here, I’d like to make a case to bring back the classics. The classics should be a part of everyone’s lives. 

« Oh but I’m mostly interested in power dynamics, and business, and sales, and non-fiction. »

Christ.
Do you imagine The Count of Monte Cristo has nothing to teach you about money?

If you want to understand love: 
Well which one?! Because Love has many textures; but lucky for you, literature has mapped them all:

Read Süskind for the obsessive kind, 

Read Hanya Yanagihara for the unbearable kind,

Read Khalil Gibran for the spiritual, luminous kind,

Read Tolstoy for the ordinary-and-therefore-DEVASTATING kind…

And Read Mary Oliver’s poems for the love that teaches you to pay attention, and to let small things change you.

And if you’re scrolling to counteract the doom acquired from scrolling, might I suggest reading
The Boy, the Mole, the Fox and the Horse.
Long live @charliemackesy. (Now some of you may not consider this a classic, but the classics weren’t classics at the time of their writing.)

See where I’m getting at?

Reading is exercise for the mind.
Reading is not a hobby. It is an necessity.
The classics are not homework. 
They are islands of knowledge you ought to take a dip in.

Ps: My book club is going to start reading a big fat mammoth classic next week if you wanna join (it’s obvs free)
