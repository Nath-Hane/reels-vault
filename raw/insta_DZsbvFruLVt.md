## Description
After exploring digital worlds, time travel, and family dramas, Japanese director Mamoru Hosoda took a turn toward dark fantasy with ‘Scarlet’ ✨

The film follows a warrior princess who embarks on a quest for revenge after the murder of her father. Loosely inspired by Hamlet, the story combines action, supernatural elements, and a reflection on hatred, forgiveness, and the consequences of living trapped by the desire for revenge. Scarlet also marked a different visual approach for Hosoda, blending advanced digital techniques with an aesthetic that moves away from both traditional 2D anime and Hollywood-style CGI ⚔️ 

Released in 2025, the film stands as one of the most ambitious and darkest works of Hosoda’s career, a filmmaker known for titles such as The Girl Who Leapt Through Time, Summer Wars, Wolf Children, Mirai, and Belle. 🎥
