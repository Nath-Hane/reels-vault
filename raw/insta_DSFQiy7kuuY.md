## Description
Butter Chicken Soup 🧈🍗🇮🇳 | Soups So Good It Can Cure Any Sickness Ep. 24

*my take on butter chicken but in soup form!

INGREDIENTS: 
Chicken Marinade:
- chicken thighs
- 2 tbsp ginger garlic paste
- 1 tsp kashmiri chili powder
- 1 tsp turmeric powder
- 2 tbsp garam masala
- 1 tsp orange food coloring (optional)
- 1/2 cup of ghee
- 1 cup yogurt
- Salt to taste

Butter Chicken:
- Ghee
- 2 onions, chopped
- 1 tsp cardamom
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 2 tbsp ginger garlic paste
- 1 tsp kashmiri chili powder
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- ½ cup cashews
- 1 ½ cup of crushed tomatoes
- Chicken stock as needed
- 1 cup of cooking cream 
- 6 cubes butter
- 1 tbsp fenugreek leaves 
- Salt & pepper to taste

Garnish:
- Cilantro
- Croutons 

*all measurements are estimates. Measure w your ❤️

INSTRUCTIONS:
- Marinate the chicken with everything under “Chicken Marinade.” Heat ghee and sear the chicken until fully cooked. Remove, chop into bite-sized pieces, and set aside.
- In the same pot, sauté the onions until softened. Stir in the spices — cardamom, cumin, ginger-garlic paste, chili powder, coriander, and garam masala l
- Add the cashews, crushed tomatoes, and chicken stock, then bring to a simmer for 10–15 minutes.
- Stir in the cooking cream, butter, fenugreek, and season with salt and pepper. Mix until fully combined and cook for another 15 mins
- return the chopped chicken to the pot 
- Serve and enjoy!
