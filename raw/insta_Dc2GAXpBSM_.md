***IMPORTANT COMMENTS***
1. As mentioned in my part one video, I use DaVinci Resolve to edit and color grade all of my videos. If you’re new to color grading or editing in general, I highly recommend DaVinci as a starting point since it’s free and there are a ton of free resources online too!
2. A couple of things I forgot to mention: 1. I’m color grading on a Mac, so my color science is set to DaVinci YRGB, and my timeline color space is set to Rec.709-A. 2. I am using the built-in film emulation LUTs in DaVinci, so you should also have access to all of them as well! Also, if the HSV or RGB Mixer feels too strong, go into Key -> Key Output and lower the gain to reduce the strength.
3. Lastly!!! I know I went through the tutorial pretty quickly, but if you guys just want to skip the entire color grading process, I made a customizable LUT for exactly that. I spent a lot of time making it an easy drag-and-drop, so if you want something quick and easy, definitely check it out. And as always, if any questions pop up, feel free to comment, and I’ll do my best to help!
