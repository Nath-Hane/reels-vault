# Video by thatfoodiejess

## Description
chicken “katsu” curry ramyun for these rainy days 

the days are getting gloomier and all i want is a warm bowl of noods, who’s with me

ALSO I wrote “katsu” cause the chicken isnt breaded in panko nor did i make it, just airfried some frozen chicken from Aldi 🙆🏽‍♀️ think smart not hard 

ingredients:
1 shin ramyun packet (or any instead noodles) + around 1/2 tsp of seasoning packet (you can leave out if you don’t want some spice)
crispy chicken - or can pan fry some protein like beef, chicken, pork
1 carrot, chopped 
1/2 onion, sliced 
1 spring onion, sliced 
1 curry block - I used s&b golden curry hot 
2 cups water 
1/2 tsp dashi powder 
optional - 1/2 tsp soy & 1/2 tsp mirin (I usually add but forgot this time 😗, still tasted bomb tho)

-In pot, add oil and fry onions and carrots for 1-2 minutes until softened a bit
-add hot water and dashi powder, bring to boil
-add 1/2 tsp of noodle packet seasoning and noodles
-cook around 2 minutes or until almost done 
-slice curry block and mix into noodles until combined 
-top with crispy chicken, spring onions and enjoy
