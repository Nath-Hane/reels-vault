Flourless • Sugar-Free • No-Bake Brownie 🍓🍫🫠

Honestly, one of the best-looking and healthiest desserts ever…

Ingredients
 - 2 bananas
 - 1 tbsp cocoa powder
 - 4 tbsp melted sugar-free dark chocolate
(feel free to add more — always a good idea 🤭)

Topping
Strawberries, raspberries & blueberries

Instructions

Follow the steps in the video:
Line a container with cling film and place the berries at the bottom.
Blend the bananas, cocoa, and melted chocolate in a food processor, then pour the mixture over the fruit.
Freeze until set.
Flip before serving.
Cutting it into neat squares looks so good 🤍

Enjoy! 🍓
