## Transcription audio
Would you like to dance with me? Why? So this is my favorite scene for my short film and it's honestly a miracle how it came to be. Yes I love this song, sorry. So in this scene Kendall has convinced his childhood crush who has a boyfriend to dance with him before he moves. And we only booked Graham who plays Kendall a week before the shoot so we had no time to rehearse and no budget for a choreographer. So I sent him a breakdown video of my brother and I dancing to the song the way I wanted the character to. And from that, Graham instantly got it and was just so charming. And that's perfectly balanced vibe. Hey my name is Jordana Summer. She was the only audition that nailed being resistant yet disarmed and what's crazy is that these two had no time to figure out that chemistry. Literally as the crew was setting up the scene was the only time we had to figure it out and just three takes to shoot it. But that's a testament to how talented the cast and the crew are to have made this special moment feel so damn sweet.
