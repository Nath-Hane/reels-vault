## Description
Korean Fried Chicken recipe👇

(Ingredients & recipe)
	•	500g chicken (wings or boneless pieces)
	•	Salt & pepper (MSG optional)
	•	chicken bouillon & garlic/ginger

(For batter)
	•	1/2 cup corn/potato starch
	•	1/4 cup cold water

(For sauce)
	•	2 tbsp gochujang
	•	1 tbsp soy sauce & gochugal
	•	1 tbsp honey or sugar
	•	2 tbsp mirin

(Simple Steps:)
	1.	Season Chicken:
	•	Toss chicken with salt, pepper, bouillon, garlic, and your choice of aromatic

	2.	Make Batter:
	•	Mix cornstarch and water into a thick slurry.

	3.	Fry:
	•	pour your batter into chicken then drain excess off the chicken, coat them with extra corn/potato starch. fry until golden and crispy.

	4.	Make Sauce:
	•	Mix sauce ingredients in a pan and simmer for 2 minutes or until desire texture

	5.	Toss & Serve:
	•	Coat fried chicken in sauce. Then garnish if you like.
