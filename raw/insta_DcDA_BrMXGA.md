# Video by nigel.tatem

## Description
I did AI research at MIT and co-authored a technical paper on AI data-center sustainability & infrastructure. I’m currently studying CS @ Cornell.

Research doesn’t need a lab or a PhD. Every tool below is free and public, similar or exact to what is used in formal research.

Here are 10 projects that will stand out for research programs and technical roles on your resume -

interpretability:

1. Find induction heads in GPT-2. The classic first mech interp result. There’s a demo notebook that gets you there in an afternoon.
Tech: Python, TransformerLens.

2. Replicate the IOI circuit. Reverse-engineer how a model figures out who “him” refers to. Activation patching and logit attribution, the core toolkit.
Tech: TransformerLens, PyTorch.

3. Linear probe. Train a classifier on a model’s hidden states to detect whether a fact is encoded before it’s spoken.
Tech: scikit-learn, TransformerLens.

4. Steering vector. Find the activation direction for a trait, inject it at inference, watch the output shift. Neuronpedia lets you try it in-browser first.
Tech: PyTorch, Neuronpedia, open-weight model.

training:

5. Train GPT-2 from scratch. Karpathy’s nanoGPT. One consumer GPU, a few hours.
Tech: PyTorch, nanoGPT, FineWeb.

6. Enter the NanoGPT speedrun. An open leaderboard for training GPT-2 to a fixed loss as fast as possible. It’s gone from 45 minutes to under 90 seconds and every record is public.
Tech: PyTorch, modded-nanogpt.

7. Scaling law. Train the same tiny model at five sizes, plot loss vs parameters, get a straight line on a log plot.
Tech: PyTorch, matplotlib.

8. Ablation study. Delete one component at a time. Measure what breaks.
Tech: PyTorch, wandb.

evaluation:

9. Build an eval nobody’s built. 100 test cases on a narrow skill plus the graders. Original benchmarks are genuinely scarce.
Tech: Python, pytest, model APIs.

10. Contamination check. Rewrite benchmark questions yourself and see if the score drops. If it does, the model memorized.
Tech: Python, any model API.

final: 

Write four pages on one of them. Abstract, method, results, limitations.
Tech: Overleaf, LaTeX, matplotlib.

Follow for more!
