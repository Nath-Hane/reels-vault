Regardless of how you feel about the practicality of this whole model, I actually think this is a really cool free recipe generator.

You know when you have random leftovers in the fridge and ingredients that don’t really make sense together? You can throw them into the website, and it helps generate recipe ideas around them.

The interesting part is how the model understands which ingredients “go together.” It maps each ingredient into a 300-dimensional vector based on two things:

1. Co-occurrence in recipes, basically what ingredients humans already tend to use together.

2. Shared flavor compounds from FlavorDB and FlavorGraph 

so it’s not just guessing based on recipes, it also considers food chemistry.
