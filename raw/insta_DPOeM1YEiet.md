Peanut Butter Miso Cookie 🥜🧈 
— simple cookie recipes ! 

INGREDIENTS 
- 115gr unsalted butter
- 100gr gula
- 150gr brown sugar
- 60gr peanut butter 
- 2-3 tbsp miso paste
- 1 telur
- 1/2 sdt vanilla extract
- 225gr tepung terigu 
- 1/2 sdt baking soda
- 1/2 sdt baking powder
- 1/2 sdt garam
- White Chocolates & Walnuts (optional)
