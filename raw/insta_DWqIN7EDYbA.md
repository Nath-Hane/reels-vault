## Transcription audio
So in a world that is becoming so AI dominated, the most important trait that is going to save you is becoming someone who has good taste. So what do I actually mean by taste? Taste is quite literally the ability to look at something and know whether it feels good or bad. It is the ability to look at a design and know if it's bad or not. It's the ability to look at a concept and know if it's fully developed or if it still needs a lot of work. So I was looking at this Y Combinator podcast and they were talking about how pretty much any venture capitalist can look at a website and know whether it was fully vibe coded or whether it wasn't. And this is becoming such a problem where everything is AI and the only way to stand out is to actually do something that is tasteful. So how do you actually develop good taste? Because it is a trait that you can develop. So number one is study things that are done well and think why is this actually done well? So look at successful companies, look at big brands and think, what are they doing that is so much better than me? And then number two is the opposite of that. Look at bad stuff. Like you need to be able to look at something that is bad and then figure out why it's off. Like look at something that is done not well and then think about what you would change about it. And then number three is surround yourself with people who have good taste. You probably have people in your life who are generally right about things. Think about why they have good taste and why they are right about things. So I run one of the largest vibe coding communities in the world. I see hundreds of projects every single week and the thing that is actually separating the people who gain traction and who have good ideas and are getting users to the people who aren't is having good taste. It is being able to look at your design and know if it's bad or not. It is being able to think of your idea objectively and know if it is a good idea or not. If you have any questions about this stuff or want to talk to real developers or other vibe coders about what you're doing, comment, taste, and I'll invite you to my community where we talk about this stuff.
