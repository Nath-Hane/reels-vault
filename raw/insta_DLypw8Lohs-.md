Past Lives (2023) is a heartfelt romantic/drama about two childhood friends, Nora and Hae Sung, who are separated when Nora’s family moves from South Korea to Canada. Directed by Celine Song in her debut feature, the movie is praised for its emotional depth, beautiful cinematography, and touching performances by Greta Lee and Teo Yoo .
