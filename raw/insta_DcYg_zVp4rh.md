I speak 6 to 8 languages, only fluent in 2, but you literally have no accent. You can hear the Ausprache so wootsign. You can best a dutch out of 6. The French is muffed, not signed. Don't pick your breath, it's not moving well. Why are your pronunciation so bad? You really look like you're going to die. These are 10 things I do to sound fluent. Check what sounds don't exist in your language, then look up the IPA. Not a language, no worries. Study the speech organs. Watch a native speaker's mouth, their lips, teeth, tongue placement, and copy that. You don't have to be creepy, they have videos on YouTube. Study phonology. Every language has rules about how sounds change when letters are written in clusters. Intonation is key. Listen to it as much as you can and track where the pitch goes up and down. Steal the rhythm. Once you know the intonation, now you just need to know the right timing and speed to stress things. Learn chunks of words, phrases, not just isolated words. Memorizing the useful package is better than adding a line in your dictionary. Shadow out loud. Exaggerate everything based on your level. Start slow, then increase the pace as you go. Record yourself and compare to natives. Practice, practice, practice. Start speaking now so you know what you don't know. Perfection only comes from fixing mistakes.
