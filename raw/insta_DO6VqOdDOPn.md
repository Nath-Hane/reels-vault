# Video by thatfoodiejess

## Description
airfryer crispy soy chicken thighs served with rice & ginger scallion oil 🐓

chicken & rice is love, is lyfe. this is all i have to say, enough the recipe 😌

ingredients:

Ginger scallion oil:
½ inch ginger, minced
4-5 scallions, minced
pinch salt
1 tsp sesame oil
1 cup hot neutral oil
- add all ingredients except sesame oil & salt into a heat-proof bowl, then pour hot oil
- add sesame oil and salt & mix

marinade ingredients:
2 Chicken thigh with skin, deboned 
½ tsp dark soy sauce
½ tsp turmeric 
1 tsp garlic paste 
1/4 tsp Black pepper
1 tbsp fish sauce 
½ tbsp light soy sauce
- then coat with cornstarch before placing in airfryer 
- cook in preheated airfryer at 195-200°C (adjust to how hot your airfryer gets) for 20 mins, turning half way

rice (i use the finger trick, add 2 rice cooker cups, then add enough broth until it reaches finger line on index finger when u place it on top of the level rice) - around 1:1 ratio:
sushi rice
chicken broth
1 spring onion, minced
