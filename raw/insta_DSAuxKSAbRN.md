## Description
Chicken Fajita Crunchwrap✨you loved my Chicken Caesar Crunchwrap, so I wanted to share a fajita version! I get my marinated fajita meat from my local Mexican market, but you can also use fajita seasoned chicken thighs. Let me know if you make!

•fajita chicken 
•chipotle crema
•shredded cheese
•tostada shell
•crema Mexicana
•sliced avocado
•romaine lettuce
•pico de gallo
•chopped cilantro
•queso fresco
•poblano sauce
•XL burrito tortilla 
•small tortilla (optional)

Cook marinated fajita chicken in a skillet until it gets a nice char on both sides & dice.

Layer in the center of tortilla: chipotle crema, chicken, cheese, tostada, crema Mexicana, avocado, lettuce, pico, cilantro, poblano sauce & small tortilla (if needed to close)

Fold tortilla in a circular motion, bringing edges toward the center. Place small tortilla in the center to seal gaps.

In a nonstick skillet over medium heat, sprinkle shredded cheese in a circular shape & let it crisp slightly. Carefully place the folded side of the Crunchwrap down onto the cheese. Gently press & flip to crisp on other side (1 minute).
