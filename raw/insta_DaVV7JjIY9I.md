## Description
Fable 5 didn’t raise the bar.

It exposed it.

Give the same model to ten people and you’ll get ten completely different results.

Not because the AI changed.

Because the thinking behind it did.

Most people expect a better model to magically produce better websites.

It won’t.

Without a real brief, the AI fills in the blanks with the most average solution it knows.

That’s why so many AI-built sites feel identical.

Better tools don’t fix weak inputs.

Better workflows do.

The biggest upgrade isn’t the model.

It’s learning how to tell the model exactly what to build.

That’s the workflow I use on every project.

I put the whole thing together in a free guide, including the prompts behind it.
