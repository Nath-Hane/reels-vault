## Description
Build your tolerance ⤵️

Yes, your urge to breath isn’t because your running out of oxygen. It’s the levels CO2 in your blood telling your brain it’s time to breathe 💨 

And GUESS WHAT? As freedivers we train our CO2 tolerance so that our urge to breathe comes later. Save this for later 💾

We can train this doing CO2 Tables: (always out of the water!)

Step 1: Find your baseline
- Lay down and relax.
- Take an inhale and hold your breathe
- Don’t force it. Stop at the first strongest urge.
- Note down your time.

Step 2: Train at 50%
- Halve your initial time
- This is your CO2 training hold

Step 3: Simple CO2 table
- Lay down and relax for 2 minutes
- Relax doing normal breathing
- Take an inhale and hold your breathe
- Hold for 50% of your initial time
- Rest for 2 minutes
- Repeat 6-8 times
- Each round, shorten the rest by 15sec

Additional Tips 💡
- Practice dry only in a comfortable space
- Inhale into both the belly and the chest
- Don’t inhale 100%, maybe 90% full, you want to be comfortable during your hold
- Stop with major discomfort
- The goal is calm comfort, not pushing
- Only practice freediving with proper training 
- never hyperventilate, this can cause blackout and it's very unsafe

NEVER PRACTICE IN THE WATER ALONE 🚨
FREEDIVING TRAINING ESSENTIAL BEFORE TRYING ANY BREATH HOLD IN WATER 

By doing CO2 tables we are teaching our nervous system that the urge to breathe isn’t an emergency, instead we are showing that we are in control, we are safe.
