Cheesy Samyang 🧀🍜 
— my favorite samyang hack !

INGREDIENTS 
- 1 pack of samyang 
- 50-60ml milk 
- Mozzarella cheese
- Topping : sausage, gyoza & eggs 

Steps
Cook your choice of Samyang noodles until they’re halfway done. Drain the water, leaving about 1–2 tablespoons in the pot. Add all the sauce packets along with some milk, then stir and cook until the noodles are fully coated in the sauce. Top with melted mozzarella cheese and serve immediately with your favorite toppings. Enjoy <3
