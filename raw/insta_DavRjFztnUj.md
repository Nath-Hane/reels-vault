Recipe:
-prepare your coffee:
* lungo (large espresso, needs to be hot)
* piece of butter (roughly a tsp)
* 0.5 oz/ 15ml maple syrup (you can add more if you like sweetness)
* pinch of salt
* froth till butter dissolved 
- add ice to serving glass
- pour in 3-4 oz/ 90-120 ml of your milk of choice
- pour your espresso mixture on top
- optionally sprinkle a little more salt on top 

That’s it & trust me it’s DELICIOUS! 🙏🏽
