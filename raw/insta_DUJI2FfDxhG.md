## Description
Korean Popcorn Chicken! 🍿🍗

#asianfood #chicken #friedchicken #koreanfood #ricebowl 

For the Chicken:
1lb Chicken Thighs (1 inch dice)
1 egg
3 Tbsp Flour
3 Tbsp Cornstarch
1/2 tsp salt, pepper
1 tsp garlic powder
1 tsp ginger powder
Vegetable oil for frying
For the Sauce:
1/4 cup ketchup
1 Tbsp soy sauce (low sodium)
1 tsp sesame oil
1 Tbsp gochujang
2 tsp rice vinegar
1/4 cup brown sugar
2 tbsp butter
