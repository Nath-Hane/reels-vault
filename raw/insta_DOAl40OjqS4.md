If you want to get into color grading and ditch those shitty ass cap cut filters here, the three things you can do right now to start. 1. Install DaVinci Resolve. I swear to God, if you are trying to color grade your show with Premiere, you are wasting the potential of your footage. The software is literally free, and if you wanted to get the studio version, it is a one-time payment. Lock in. 2. Get your hands on some log or raw footage. Either you film your own footage, or you borrow some. A lot of the big brand name camera manufacturers like RE and RED have free test footage that you can download, and even YouTube has some hidden bangers. Not gonna lie, this is literally a cheat code when it comes to color grading, and it is 100% free since it comes with Resolve. You essentially already get a look with the 2383 LUT right out of the box, and you can make some fine-tuned adjustments to add your own flavor. It gives you such a nice foundation to start on, and it's pretty much what I've used to color grade all my videos.
