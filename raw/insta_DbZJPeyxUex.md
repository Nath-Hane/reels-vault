I think I have the nerdiest audience on the internet which is fantastic because I think I've found the best way for you to be learning anything if you want to have a little bit of a challenge and something tangible at the end. Let me introduce you to the world of Olympiads. Olympiads are very hard exams aimed at students and they cover a lot of different subjects but why am I telling you about this? I'm telling you about this because they all have world-class teaching materials that you can access online for free and then a bunch of past papers and exams that you can then take to check your learning. It's such a fantastic addition to a personal curriculum so let me share some of my favorites. The linguistic Olympiad is probably the best one in terms of resources and you might be sat there thinking, hold on, don't I need to know a bunch of languages? No you don't. It's actually about pattern recognition a lot more than anything else and you can have a look on international linguistic Olympiad pages or websites such as this one which provide practice problems that you can go through and work solutions and then you can go on and take past papers. For the international economics Olympiad, again there are past papers and sample questions on the website but good resources to supplement this with are the economics courses on MIT OpenCourseWare or things like Marginal Revolution University which have a lot of content. If you would love to punish yourself then feel free to try the International Maths Olympiad. You'll probably have to do a lot of requisite maths but if you look at like national Olympiads first you can kind of go through a lot of work problem solutions. There's a lot of YouTube channels that are completely dedicated to taking you through IMO problems. Similarly for those pain-seeking individuals the International Physics Olympiad has a lot of practice problems on the website. There is the Feynman Lectures which is probably the best baseline that you can be looking at for physics understanding. The world's best free education is hiding in competitions and now you know how to find it. And if you care about becoming a little more intellectual then make sure you follow along for more.
